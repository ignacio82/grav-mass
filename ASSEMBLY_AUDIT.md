## 1. Confirmed Bug Reproduction & Keyhole Fix (Phases 1 & 2)

### 1.1 Root Cause (`pre-fix`, commit `0b63952`)
In `cad/trunk.py`, `make_female_dovetail_slot_cutter()` created a closed `45°` dovetail pocket (`7.0 mm` outer neck, `16.0 mm` deep floor base, `4.5 mm` radial depth, `18.0 mm` vertical slot height) topped directly by a `45°` inward-sloping roof ramp (`Z = 18.0..22.5 mm`). `make_male_dovetail_tenon()` in `cad/brackets.py` builds a `15.40 mm` wide (`4.35 mm` deep, `17.75 mm` tall) dovetail tenon.
- Sweeping rigid insertion axes in `<= 0.50 mm` steps (`cad/test_bracket_insertion.py` on tag `pre-fix`):
  - **Stacked trunk (`trunk_base + trunk_mid + trunk_top`):** **0 / 24 PASS** (`verified by computation`). Radial `+X` blocked at `dx = 0.40 mm` (`18.41–18.49 mm³` overlap on cardinal/60° stations, `11.89 mm³` on `180°` conduit stations); vertical `±Z` bounded to `<= 0.5 mm` travel (`+0.5/-3.0 mm` on `180°`).
  - **Unstacked `trunk_base` (`0..150 mm`):** **Only 3 / 24 PASS** (`verified by computation`): stations `St 8 (480°)`, `St 19 (1140°) -> Z=150 seam`, `St 20 (1200°)` crossed `Z = 150` or `Z = 300` open seams; **all 7 stations wholly inside `trunk_base` (`k = 1..7`, `60°..420°`) failed 7 / 7**.

### 1.2 Keyhole Drop-In Fix & Cantilever Strength (`cad/common_params.py`, `cad/trunk.py`, `cad/brackets.py`)
- **Geometry (`DOVETAIL_ENTRY_W = 16.20 mm`, `DOVETAIL_ENTRY_DEPTH = 4.85 mm`, `DOVETAIL_ENTRY_H = 18.60 mm`, `DOVETAIL_ENTRY_PEAK_W = 1.60 mm`, `DOVETAIL_DETENT_Y_HALF = 3.44 mm`):**

  - Solid volumes (`verified by computation`): `bracket_01 = 17.62 cm³`, `track_seg_01 = 187.72 cm³`, `planet_neptune = 10.71 cm³`.
