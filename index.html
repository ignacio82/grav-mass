📖 How to Measure Gravity (g) with Your Printed Model

Like Galileo's inclined plane, the 4.5-turn conical spiral track dilutes vertical free-fall (Δh = 348.9 mm) over a 2,868.3 mm helical path (~7.0° slope), slowing the descent to ~4.18 s so you can accurately measure Earth's gravity g ≈ 9.81 m/s²:

  1. Built-In Physical Scales: The outer track rail has 6 Timing Gates (G0–G5) with top-rim V-notches, 100 mm spiral distance ticks (s), and 0.35 mm acoustic click ribs inside the trough floor. The front trunk windows have 25 mm vertical elevation ticks (Δh).
  2. 3 Ways to Time Marble Drops (Δt):
    • 📱 Phyphox App (±1 ms Acoustic Stopwatch): Run the free Phyphox app beside the tree—the 0.35 mm floor ribs emit crisp audio clicks at G0–G5. Enter your measured Δt above and click + Log Δt (s).
    • 🎥 Slow-Mo Video (120 / 240 fps): Film the front -Y spine where G1, G2, G3, G4 are aligned vertically (Δt = Δframes / fps).
    • ⏱️ Live Stopwatch: Click ⏱️ Tap Start Gate as a marble crosses your start gate and 🛑 Tap Finish Gate at the end gate. Repeat N = 5–10 times to average out reaction time by 1/√N!
  3. Galilean Inclined-Spiral Formula & Core Inertia (κ):
    ĝ = [ 2 · κ · Δs · (√s_B − √s_A)² ] / [ Δh · (Δt̄)² ] (From rest at G0 where s_A = 0: ĝ = 2 · κ · s² / (Δh · Δt̄²))
    Because a rolling sphere splits potential energy mgΔh into translational + rotational kinetic energy, select your marble's rotational inertia factor κ = 1 + I/(m·r_eff²) above: 1.46 (LED + M8 Nut), 1.42 (12.7 mm Steel Ball), or 1.54 (Hollow PLA + LED).

    📌 Complete Pin-by-Pin Connection Map (12V Single-Cable + 5V Buck + A092 + C008)
    ⚪ White (G26)
    Zone 2 — Helical Track Pebble Wire (B0CCS11V7H, 50 LEDs): Plugs into the Center DATA pin of the Track Wire's 3-pin JST-SM connector (routed through cob_wire_conduit).
    🟡 Yellow (G32)
    Zone 1 — Apple Topper 16-LED Ring (B09YTMNGTD): Plugs into the Center DATA pin of the 1 m 3-pin JST-SM extension cable (B0CT827QQC) threaded straight down the 10×8 mm rear (-X) trunk conduit.
    🔴 +5V Buck (WAGO-3)
    Dedicated 12V → 5.0V 5A Buck Converter Rail (WAGO-3): Powers both the Track Wire (start & end power-injection leads) and Topper Ring with zero voltage sag. Leave the A096 Grove Red (+5V) wire CAPPED / UNCONNECTED so the external 5V buck never backfeeds A092's internal 5V regulator!
    ⚫ Star GND (WAGO-2/4)
    Common Star Ground (WAGO-2 & WAGO-4): Ties the rear 12V DC Barrel Jack (J1), A092 GND, A096 Grove Black (GND), the 5V Buck Converter -OUT, and both LED ground leads to a single common reference.
    🔩 12V VIN & M1/M2
    Single 12V DC Rear Barrel Jack (J1) & N20 Auger Motor: Rear -X 5.5×2.1mm jack feeds WAGO-1 (+12V_BUS) (via 5A fuse F0) → A092 VIN/GND (via 2A fuse F1, powering both A092 and C008). Clamp a 12V 150 RPM N20 motor (Option B) into A092 M1/M2 (or use a 12V→6V buck + external H-bridge for a 6V LA002 motor in Option A).
