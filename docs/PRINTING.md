# 🖨️ 3D Printing & Support Guide (18 Pre-Arranged `.3mf` Build Plates — Snapmaker U1 `0.4 mm` Nozzle, Zero Collisions, Max 4 Colors Per Plate)

All ready-to-print `.3mf` files are located in [`output/3mf/`](../output/3mf/), pre-configured for the **Snapmaker U1 with a `0.4 mm` nozzle** (`printer_settings_id = "Snapmaker U1 (0.4 nozzle)"`, `nozzle_diameter = ["0.4", "0.4", "0.4", "0.4"]`, `0.20 mm` standard layer height), and use Snapmaker OrcaSlicer's native `5 × 4` multi-plate grid (`324.0 mm` X/Y plate stride centered at `+135.0 mm`) with unified multi-part objects (`<components>` + `<part subtype="normal_part">`). **Every plate contains only the needed parts (including multiple copies of parts used multiple times, such as 3× `Conduit_Cover_Strip`, 24× `bracket_01..24`, and 12× `apple_hemisphere_a/b_01..06`), uses at most 4 colors (4 toolheads), fits comfortably inside a `231 × 231 mm` centered zone (`>= 19.6 mm` bed margin on all 4 sides), has zero 2D Convex-Hull / AABB overlaps (`>= 12.5 mm` part clearance), and Plates 1–15 are 100% single-color plates with `enable_prime_tower = 0`!**

## Fit Validation & Retrofit Tools (`Test_Coupons.3mf` + Cutting Jig)

Before committing multi-hour plates, print **[`output/3mf/Test_Coupons.3mf`](../output/3mf/Test_Coupons.3mf)** (`27` labeled coupons, `218.7 × 150.0 × 54.0 mm` footprint, `>= 8.8 mm` clearance) across `0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25 mm` radial/clearance steps for all 5 joint classes:

1. **Keyhole Bracket Socket + Tenon** (`Coupon_Socket_Keyhole` + `Coupon_Tenon_c10..c25` — **print first** to confirm keyhole L-drop & neck bump tightness on your printer).
2. **Track Scarf Peg/Socket** (`Coupon_ScarfSock_c10..c25` + `Coupon_ScarfPeg`).
3. **Bayonet Marble A/B** (`Coupon_Bayonet_A_c10..c25` + `Coupon_Bayonet_B`).
4. **Trunk Stack Keyed Tenon/Socket** (`Coupon_TrunkSock_c10..c25` + `Coupon_TrunkTenon`).
5. **Planet Pedestal Peg + LED Socket** (`Coupon_PlanetSock_c10..c25` + `Coupon_PlanetPeg`).

- **Pre-Fix Trunk Retrofit Tool:** If you already printed `trunk_base` before the keyhole update, slice and print **[`output/Trunk_Keyhole_Cutting_Jig.stl`](../output/Trunk_Keyhole_Cutting_Jig.stl)** (`0.20 mm` layer, `100%` infill or `6` walls in PLA/PETG) and follow **Step 5** in [`docs/ASSEMBLY.md`](ASSEMBLY.md) to saw open sockets `k = 1..7` into drop-in entry windows.

> **💡 Pitch-Leveled Horizontal Track Orientation (`Option A`) + `"On build plate only"`:**  
> Every helical track segment (`track_seg_01`..`14`) is **pitch-tilted by its helical chord angle (`~6.4°–12.9°`)** so **both its downhill end and uphill end rest near `Z = 0.0`** (`track_seg_01` & `track_seg_14_top_entry` need brim/tree contact at endcaps per [`ASSEMBLY_AUDIT.md`](../ASSEMBLY_AUDIT.md))—dropping segment print height down to `~35.0 mm`. Enabling **"On build plate only"** and **"Don't support bridges"** keeps the `45°` underside LED dovetail channel, snap-lock sockets, and bracket peg holes free of support material.
