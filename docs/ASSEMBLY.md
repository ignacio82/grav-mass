## 🔢 Hidden Inner Assembly Numbering System (Zero Visible Marks When Assembled!)
Every multi-piece subsystem has its **assembly order number debossed into an internal/mating surface** so you can identify every part at a glance during assembly, while **100% of the numbers are hidden inside joints, sockets, or bores once assembled**:

| Subsystem | Engraved Mark | Where to Find It (Before Assembly) | How It Hides (Once Assembled) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Apple Marble Hemispheres** | **`A`**, **`B`** | Inside the hollow central core ceiling (`Z = 10.6 mm`) of `Apple_Hemisphere_A` and `Apple_Hemisphere_B` | Sealed inside the `Ø 25.0 mm` sphere once `A` and `B` lock together |
| **Archimedes Auger Halves** | **`1`**, **`2`** | **`1`** on the flat top of `auger_lower`'s male hex peg (`Z = 208 mm`); **`2`** on the flat top of `auger_upper`'s shaft (`Z = 198 mm`) | **`1`** hides inside `auger_upper`'s bottom hex socket; **`2`** hides deep inside the top of `trunk_top` |

## Step 1: Assemble the 6 Glowing Rolling Apple Marbles (`A` + `B` Quarter-Turn Bayonet Twist-Lock)
1. Take the 6 pairs of [`Apple_Hemisphere_A`](../output/Apple_Hemisphere_A.stl) (marked **`A`** inside the central cavity, featuring the **3-lug male bayonet collar** `R_neck = 10.30 mm` / `R_lug = 11.24 mm`, `36°` wide lugs with a `45°` elephant's-foot relief chamfer) and [`Apple_Hemisphere_B`](../output/Apple_Hemisphere_B.stl) (marked **`B`** inside the central cavity, featuring the **3 wide vertical `44°` entry keyway slots and clockwise undercut locking tunnels**).
2. **Insert the Y-Shape 3-LED Light (`KC013`):**
   - Drop a **`Ø 18.0 × 6.5 mm` [Bambu Lab Y-Shape LED Light with Magnetic Control Switch (`KC013`)](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/y-shape-led-light-with-magnetic-control-switch)** directly into the **`Ø 18.70 × 7.30 mm`** equatorial chamber of `Apple_Hemisphere_B`. Its 3 Y-spaced LEDs illuminate the entire thin-shell hollow marble, while its steel `CR1632` battery provides centered rolling momentum (no extra weights or nuts needed).
3. **Drop In & Quarter-Turn Twist to Lock:** Align the **3 male bayonet lugs** on `Apple_Hemisphere_A` with the **3 open vertical keyway slots** on `Apple_Hemisphere_B`—the two halves drop flush together cleanly. Then **twist `Apple_Hemisphere_A` `45°` clockwise ("righty-tighty")** to lock the 3 lugs underneath the solid retaining ceiling so the halves cannot separate during rolling or drops. Repeat for all 6 marbles.

## Step 2: Install the N20 Motor, Rear 12V DC Barrel Jack (`J1`), 5V Buck Converter & M5Stack ESPHome Controller (`A092` + `C008`) in `trunk_base` (`#1`)
1. Turn [`trunk_base`](../output/trunk_base.stl) (marked **`1`** on its top tenon rim; `Ø 185.0 mm` diagonal root footprint with a cavernous **`Ø 135.0 × 50.0 mm` 45° Conical Root Vault**) upside down to access the bottom vault.
2. **Mount the Rear `-X` `12V` DC Barrel Jack (`J1`, `B07C7VSRBG`):**
   - Thread the pre-wired **`5.5 × 2.1 mm` Female Panel-Mount DC Barrel Jack (`B07C7VSRBG`, `M11 × 0.75`)** through the rear `-X` **`Ø 11.40 mm` port** (`Z = 10.0 mm`) on `trunk_base` and tighten its hex nut inside the recessed nut pocket. Route its `18 AWG` pigtails into the vault through `dc_jack_vault_portal` to `WAGO-1 (+12V_BUS)` (via inline `5A` fuse `F0`, `B087BT1PH3`) and `WAGO-2 (STAR_GND)` (`B07NKT2P2F`).
3. **Press the N20 Gear Motor (`B0BFRPMSW6`) Directly Into the Central Shroud Pocket:**
   - Slide the **`12V DC 150 RPM N20` Micro Metal Gear Motor (`B0BFRPMSW6`)** upward directly into `trunk_base`'s central **`12.8 × 10.8 × 26.2 mm` Isolated Motor Shroud Pocket** (`Ø 3.0 mm` D-shaft protruding up through the ceiling, `1.20 mm` bed floor), and route its 2-wire lead out the bottom `-Y` shroud wire exit notch (`6.5 × 7.6 mm`).
4. **Drop Boards into the Hollow Root Vault (`>= 18.0 mm` Finger Access):**
   - **Branch 1 & 3 — M5Stack Controller & Motor Driver (`A092` + `C008`):** Snap the **M5Stack ATOM Lite ESP32 (`C008`, `24.0 × 24.0 × 9.5 mm`)** onto the **Atomic H-Bridge Driver Base (`A092`, `48.0 × 24.0 × 18.0 mm`)** and seat into the stepped `-Y` cradle (`49.2 × 25.2 × 20.3 mm` at `(0, -33.0 mm)`). Connect `WAGO-1 (+12V_BUS)` (via `2A` inline fuse `F1`) and `WAGO-2 (STAR_GND)` (seated in the `+X` `19.8 × 31.2 × 18.0 mm` WAGO `221-415` organizer cradle at `(+44.5, -12.0 mm)`) to `A092` **`VIN`** and **`GND`** screw terminals (`A092`'s internal `MP1584EN` buck regulator automatically powers `C008` from `12V`—no USB-C cable needed!).
   - **Branch 2 — Dedicated UCTRONICS `12V → 5.0V 5A` LED Buck Converter (`B07XXWQ49N`):** Seat the **`UCTRONICS DC 9V–36V to 5V 5A` Screw-Terminal Buck Converter (`B07XXWQ49N` / `U6223`, `63.0 × 27.0 × 10.0 mm`)** into the dedicated `+Y` snap-in cradle (`64.2 × 28.2 × 12.0 mm` at `(-4.0, +36.5 mm)`) inside the Hollow Root Vault, fed from `WAGO-1 (+12V_BUS)` via `3A` inline fuse `F2` and `WAGO-2 (STAR_GND)` into its `VIN+` and `GND` screw terminals. Connect its `OUT+` and `GND` screw terminals (plus one `1000 µF 16V` bulk capacitor `B07WPWFSNQ`, `Ø 10.0 × 17.0 mm`) to **`WAGO-3 (+5V_LED)`** and **`WAGO-4 (GND_LED)`** (linked to `WAGO-2` Star Ground).
5. **Zero-Fuss Solder-Free Terminal Clamping & Unified Bottom Lid:**
   - Clamp the **`12V 150 RPM N20` Motor (`B0BFRPMSW6`)** leads into the **`M1` & `M2` screw terminals** on `A092`.
   - Plug the `200 mm` **M5Stack Grove2Dupont Cable (`A096`)** into `C008`'s `HY2.0-4P` Grove port. **IMPORTANT (`5V` Rail Isolation):** Leave the `A096` **`Red (+5V)` wire UNCONNECTED (cap inside an empty WAGO lever slot)** so the external `5V` LED buck converter never backfeeds `A092`'s internal `5V` regulator! Connect `A096` `Black (GND)` to `WAGO-2` (`STAR_GND`).
   - Seat [`Bottom_Electronics_Lid`](../output/Bottom_Electronics_Lid.stl) into the 4 diagonal rabbet tabs under `trunk_base` and fasten it with **`4× M2.5 × 6 mm` BHCS screws (`B0BYF4X65F`)** (`Ø 5.0 mm` center access port exposes `C008`'s status LED & `GPIO39` button while louvered vents cool the root vault).
6. From inside the top of `trunk_base`, press [`Motor_Clutch_Driver`](../output/Motor_Clutch_Driver.stl) (dark brown `T1` hex driver) down onto the N20 motor's D-shaft and lock it onto the D-flat with **`1× M2.5 × 6 mm` screw (`B0BYF4X65F`)** through the `+X` clamp pilot hole.

## Step 3: Couple & Insert the Two-Stage Archimedes Screw Auger (`#1` + `#2`)
1. Identify [`auger_lower`](../output/auger_lower.stl) (engraved with **`1`** on the flat top of its male hex peg) and [`auger_upper`](../output/auger_upper.stl) (engraved with **`2`** on the flat top of its central shaft).
2. Slide the hexagonal male peg (`#1`) at the top of `auger_lower` into the matching female hexagonal socket in the bottom of `auger_upper` (`#2`), hiding the **`1`** inside the joint and aligning the helical screw flights into one continuous spiral.
3. Lower the assembled auger into `trunk_base` so the bottom female hex socket of `auger_lower` engages `Motor_Clutch_Driver` and rests smoothly above the `Z = 37.20 mm` internal thrust shelf. Spin the auger by hand slightly to verify smooth rotation.

## Step 5: Drop In All 26 Keyhole Cantilever Branch Brackets (Sorted by Engraved `1–26` Numbers)
> **✅ Zero Conduit Interference (`150°` Relocated Wire Channel):** Because the `10.6 × 8.5 mm` trunk conduit sits at **`150°`**, none of the `60°` bracket columns (`#1..#26`) cross the wire channel—you can route the topper cable in either order without trapping the `SM2.54-3P` connector!

1. **Keyhole Drop-In Motion (Two-Step L-Path — Rigid PLA, Zero Flex):** Each trunk slot features an upper **Keyhole Entry Window** (`16.20 × 4.85 × 18.60 mm` + `45°` support-free gable roof) directly above the `18.0 mm` tapered `45°` dovetail seat. For each station **`#1` through `#26`**:
   - Align the bracket tenon with the wide upper keyhole window (`~15–16 mm` above the seat floor) and **push straight radially inward** into the trunk until flush.
   - **Slide straight downward (`-Z`) `15–16 mm`** until the dovetail tenon seats firmly into the tapered wedge and the `45°` anti-lift neck friction bump snaps past the slot mouth.
2. **Plates 3 & 4** ([`Plate_3_SingleColor_Brown_Brackets_01_To_12.3mf`](../output/3mf/Plate_3_SingleColor_Brown_Brackets_01_To_12.3mf) and [`Plate_4_SingleColor_Brown_Brackets_13_To_26.3mf`](../output/3mf/Plate_4_SingleColor_Brown_Brackets_13_To_26.3mf)) print all **26 exact-radius station brackets** ([`bracket_01`](../output/bracket_01.stl) through [`bracket_26`](../output/bracket_26.stl)) upright on `Z = 0` with zero supports:
   - **Lower Stations (`1` to `6`, Tier 4, on Plate 3):** Push into windows & drop [`bracket_01`](../output/bracket_01.stl)..[`bracket_06`](../output/bracket_06.stl) (`theta = 60°..360°`).
   - **Lower-Mid Stations (`7` to `12`, Tier 3, on Plate 3):** Push into windows & drop [`bracket_07`](../output/bracket_07.stl)..[`bracket_12`](../output/bracket_12.stl) (`theta = 420°..720°`).
   - **Upper-Mid Stations (`13` to `18`, Tier 2, on Plate 4):** Push into windows & drop [`bracket_13`](../output/bracket_13.stl)..[`bracket_18`](../output/bracket_18.stl) (`theta = 780°..1080°`).
   - **Upper & Feeder Stations (`19` to `26`, Tier 1, on Plate 4):** Push into windows & drop [`bracket_19`](../output/bracket_19.stl)..[`bracket_26`](../output/bracket_26.stl) (`theta = 1140°..1560°`).
3. **Retrofit for Already-Printed `pre-fix` `trunk_base` (`#1`, Stations `1`–`7`):**
   - If your `trunk_base` was printed before the keyhole update (`closed 45° roof` pockets at `k = 1..7`), print **[`Trunk_Keyhole_Cutting_Jig.stl`](../output/Trunk_Keyhole_Cutting_Jig.stl)** (`cad/retrofit_jig.py`), snap its registration tooth into each closed `7.0 mm` pocket mouth, saw open the `16.6 × 18.8 mm` guide window above the pocket, deburr, and drop in brackets `#1..#7`.

## Step 6: Seat Helical Track Segments (`1` to `14`) Vertically (`-Z`) via Stepped Half-Laps + Split-Tine Bracket Snap Pegs
1. Check the **`8.0°` Stepped Half-Lap Seams** at each green PETG track segment end (`track_seg_01` = `1` → `track_seg_14_top_entry` = `14`):
   - **No Orthogonal Deadlock:** Every seam uses overlapping vertical `-Z` stepped half-lap shelves aligned on the `120°, 240°, ..., 1560°` bracket stations **plus** **Twin `Ø 4.00 × 4.5 mm` Vertical Split-Tine Snap Pegs** (`u = ±10.6 mm` on brackets `01..26`) clicking straight into `Ø 4.30 mm` (`chamfered mouth`) underside track sockets! Because both the track-to-track lap shelf and the bracket snap pegs engage along **`-Z` straight downward**, every segment drops cleanly into place from `Segment 1` upward to `Segment 14`!
2. Start at the bottom with **Segment `1`** ([`track_seg_01`](../output/track_seg_01.stl)):
   - Lower Segment `1` vertically (`-Z`) onto `trunk_base` (`-90°` return chute docks flush beneath the intake window) and press its underside sockets straight down onto Tier 4 brackets **`#1` (`60°`)** and **`#2` (`120°`)**.
3. Continue upward in order from **Segment `2`** ([`track_seg_02`](../output/track_seg_02.stl)) through **Segment `14`** ([`track_seg_14_top_entry`](../output/track_seg_14_top_entry.stl)):
   - Drop each segment straight down (`-Z`) so its downhill stepped half-lap shoulder rests directly on top of the previous segment's shelf **and** all three underside socket pairs click firmly onto its seam and midpoint bracket split-tine pegs (through `bracket_26` at `1560°`!). At `1710°`, Segment `14`'s catch basin aligns directly below `trunk_top`'s `-Y` discharge spout.
4. **Snap In the Full-Length Under-Track BTF-LIGHTING `DC5V` `WS2812B` Pebble/Seed Pixel Flexible Wire (`B0CCS11V7H`, Segment `1` → Segment `14`):**
   - Seat the starter end of the **[BTF-LIGHTING `DC5V` `WS2812B` Pebble/Seed Pixel Flexible Wire (`16.4ft / 50 LEDs`, `B0CCS11V7H`)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CCS11V7H)** into the **`10.0 × 16.0 × 3.7 mm` starter feed pocket** on the underside of `track_seg_01` (`-70°`).
   - Press all `50×` oval epoxy Pebble LEDs (`6.2 × 3.2 mm`) upward through the **`4.6 mm` snap-in throat** into the deepened **`8.4 × 6.2 mm` internal dovetail chamber** (`0.7 mm` throat + `1.9 mm` `45°` flare + `3.6 mm` retention bay) all the way from `track_seg_01` to `track_seg_14_top_entry`.
   - Feed the 3-pin `JST-SM` input lead and power-injection leads through `cob_wire_entry_port` on `trunk_base` into the vault: connect `White (GPIO26)` to center `DIN`, and clamp `Red (+5V)` and `Black (GND)` into **`WAGO-3 (+5V_LED)`** and **`WAGO-4 (GND_LED)`**.
